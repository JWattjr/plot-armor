# Product
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
User specified Next.js App Router, React, strict TypeScript, genlayer-js 1.1.8 and Vercel. Four Python Intelligent Contracts on StudioNet.
## Users
Friends predicting TV episode plots, plus reviewers inspecting immutable on-chain evidence. No wallet needed to browse.
## Product Purpose
Bet on favourite shows with validator-judged compilation and settlement, while protecting unwatched episodes from spoilers.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Yes/no and 2-6 fixed options; Wikipedia episode summaries only. GEN is simulated StudioNet currency; free predictions and seasonal scores. No films, sports or chat. Never choose arbitrary winners. Full refunds at deterministic deadlines. Permanent oldid evidence. Ask owner before any deposit.
## Brand Commitments
Plot Armor. Streaming-guide look, show-poster-style cards, spoiler shield and bold outcome chips. Mobile-first.
## Evidence on Hand
No Plot Armor deployments exist at initialization. Proof must be collected from real receipts. Historical rehearsals must not be represented as competitive forecasts.
## Product Principles
Freeze before entry. Code owns money. Validators independently check evidence. Reveal only by choice.
